About This System

Our Mission

The Elderly & Disabled Helping System is designed to help elderly people and those with disabilities live more independently by connecting them with caring helpers who can assist with daily activities.

Who Can Use This System?

  • Elderly people who need assistance with daily tasks
  • People with physical disabilities
  • People who cannot walk or have difficulty walking
  • People who have difficulty using their hands
  • People with visual difficulties
  • Volunteers and caregivers who want to help others

How It Works

1

Register

Create an account as a user or helper.

2

Request or Accept

Users request help; helpers accept requests.

3

Get Help

Helpers provide assistance and mark requests complete.

Accessibility Features

This system is built with accessibility as a top priority. We use large buttons, readable fonts, high-contrast colors, and simple navigation to ensure everyone can use the platform comfortably.
